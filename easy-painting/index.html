How we prep

The part most painters rush.

Paint only lasts as long as what's underneath it. This is what happens on every house before the color goes on.

Step 01Pressure wash

Dirt, mildew and chalky old paint come off first, so the new coat sticks to the house and not to grime.

Step 02Scrape & sand

Loose and peeling paint gets scraped back and the edges sanded smooth.

Step 03Repair & caulk

Bad boards get flagged, gaps and cracks get caulked so water stays out.

Step 04Mask & prime

Windows, plants and walkways get covered. Bare wood gets primed.

Step 05Paint

Body, trim, doors and accents, with clean cut lines where the colors meet.

Step 06Walkthrough

We walk the house with you and handle touch-ups before we call it finished.
